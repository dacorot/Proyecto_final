What is RenoEnergy

Nowadays, everyone is talking about sustainable energy, but an action is worth a thousand words. Using biogas from the fermentation of organic waste, RenoEnergy engines can generate heat and electricity as a reliable energy source to power your business today, tomorrow and into the future.

Get Started

Your challenge

With the phasing out of coal and nuclear energy, centralized energy producers often replace large plants with several smaller, decentralized wind and photovoltaic plants, which provide heat and electricity where it is needed. But that has a downside: fluctuations in electricity generation occur due to the volatility of renewable energy sources. To avoid blackouts, the missing electricity must be quickly generated from other available sources. That is where biogas, another renewable energy source, comes into play, as a complement to those volatile energy sources that depend on weather conditions.

Get Started

Industry trends

Instead of producing waste that generates emissions, organic waste, also called biomass, can be used as a valuable source of energy when converted into biogas. And the use of biogas to power engines as a substitute for fossil fuels is increasing across the energy market. This is because this renewable energy source serves as a complement to other renewable energy sources such as wind and solar, with a big difference. It does not depend on weather conditions.

    About

    RenoEnergy biogas generator sets are widely used by farmers and industrial users around the world. Companies can use biogas power plants on the ground to meet their own heat and electricity needs, or biogas generator sets can produce electricity for future supply to the grid. Biogas generator engines can be installed inside buildings or can also be supplied as ready-to-use biogas power plant solutions in a container.

    The biogas generation process consists of three steps: Preparation of the bioinput, fermentation and subsequent treatment of the residual material. Initially, the organic material is collected in a primary pit, sterilized to eliminate harmful germs in the case of food waste, and transferred to the digester. The biogas produced in the digester is collected in a gas storage tank to ensure a continuous supply of gas, regardless of fluctuations in gas production. Finally, the biogas is fed to a biogas engine. For safety reasons, it is recommended to install a gas torch to burn off excess gas in case too much gas has been produced..

    Frequently Asked Questions

    The transition towards a sustainable and clean energy model is increasingly evident and necessary, which has led to a growing interest in renewable energies. At Baena Solar, we want to help you understand why renewable energies are an option for the present and future. In this article, we answer the most frequently asked questions about renewable energies, their characteristics and benefits.

    What are renewable energies?

    Renewable energies, also known as clean energies, are those that are obtained from natural sources and are inexhaustible or with a high capacity for renewal. Unlike fossil fuels, renewable energies do not emit greenhouse gases or other pollutants and are a sustainable and environmentally friendly alternative..

    solar thermal energy

    Solar thermal energy uses solar radiation to generate heat. To obtain this energy, thermal solar panels are used, which capture solar radiation and convert it into heat. This heat can be used to heat different fluids, such as air or water. Solar thermal energy is mainly used for heating and producing hot water in homes and buildings..

    What renewable energies exist?

    Renewable energies, as their name suggests, are infinite, that is, almost inexhaustible and exist in many types: wind, solar, thermal... The most used are the following.

    Passive solar energy

    Passive solar energy uses the sun's heat without the need for any mechanism. This means that it does not require the use of solar panels to take advantage. It is an architectural design that makes the most of sunlight and thermal energy from the sun to maintain a comfortable temperature inside, without the need for heating or air conditioning systems. Techniques used to construct passive solar buildings include using south-facing windows, using building materials with thermal properties, or using thermal masses to store solar energy..

    Thermoelectric solar energy

    Solar thermoelectric energy produces electricity through the use of solar concentrators that generate electricity through the production of water vapor. These systems use mirrors and lenses to concentrate solar radiation on a focal point, where a pipe containing water is located. Concentrated solar energy causes water to heat and generate steam, which drives a turbine to generate electricity.
